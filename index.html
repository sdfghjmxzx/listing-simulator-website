What is Listing Simulator?

A local workspace for the Excel catalog files Amazon gives you — without deleting sheets or uploading your workbook to the cloud.

Local & private

Open, edit, and save on your machine. Nothing is hosted on our servers. Your API keys for optional AI stay in your user profile.

Built for Amazon templates

Works with catalog reports (.xlsx, .xlsm, .xls). Find Catalog and Bind Rules keep the workbook aligned with Amazon’s sheet structure.

Clones, not raw Data rows

Edit working copies in Clones. Export builds a focused upload file for one family (or a few) — not the whole catalog at once.

Features

Everything you need from download to upload.

Inventory Manager

Families, standalones, future families, and past listings in one sortable list with health hints.

Listing editor

Edit titles, bullets, gallery, variation theme, attach children, and review listing health before export.

Merge catalog

Pull in a fresh inventory file while keeping Clones, Notes, and history until you Save.

Export clones

Generate an Amazon-ready .xlsx from checked families on the Clones sheet.

View sheets

Jump to Clones and Notes grids when you need spreadsheet-style access.

Optional AI (Aleksa)

Chat, field enhance, inventory scan, and gallery image tools when you add your own API key.

How it works

Typical flow from Seller Central to upload.

1

Get your catalog

Download an inventory / catalog report from Seller Central and save the Excel file.

2

Open & bind

Open catalog in Listing Simulator. Use Find Catalog and Bind Rules if the banners ask.

3

Edit listings

Pick a row in Inventory, create a Clone, edit on the Listing page, then Save.

4

Export & upload

Export one family (or a few) from Clones, review the file, upload to Amazon.
